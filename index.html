Scan to BIM Workflow

How Scan to BIM Works

A proven four-stage engineering process that converts physical spaces into intelligent, parametric BIM models with millimetric accuracy.

01 ⌖

Capture Existing Building

Scan an existing building using high-precision laser scanners, LiDAR, or similar reality-capture technology to record all physical surfaces.

02 ⌘

Create Point Cloud

Generate and register a dense point cloud containing millions of accurate 3D measurement points representing the exact geometry of the building.

03 ▦

BIM Conversion

Convert the processed scan and point cloud into an intelligent parametric BIM model using industry-leading software such as Autodesk Revit.

04 ◇

Accurate 3D Deliverables

Deliver an accurate 3D model detailing walls, floors, ceilings, doors, windows, and other architectural and structural building elements.

Geospatial Intelligence

Geospatial Mapping Capabilities

From ground level to aerial and orbital viewpoints, we provide spatial intelligence to understand, analyse, and plan across landscapes.

⚲

1. GIS Mapping

Creating digital maps showing roads, land boundaries, buildings, utilities, topography, and other rich geographic information layers for planning and asset management.

✈

2. Drone Surveying & Mapping

Using drones to capture high-resolution aerial images and create land maps, digital terrain models (DTM), digital elevation models (DEM), and textured 3D surface models.

🛰

3. Satellite Image Analysis

Studying land use, urban development, vegetation health, water bodies, environmental monitoring, and historical changes in the landscape using multi-spectral satellite imagery.
